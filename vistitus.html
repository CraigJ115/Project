Visit Us

The V&A is the world's leading museum of art, design and performance. Come and see us in South Kensington, London.

Plan Your Visit

📍 Address

Cromwell Road
South Kensington
London SW7 2RL

Nearest tube: South Kensington

🎟️ Admission

Free entry to the permanent collection. Charges apply for some temporary exhibitions. Children under 6 are always free.

♿ Accessibility

The museum is fully accessible. Wheelchairs are available to borrow. Audio guides and large print guides are available on request.

🍽️ Food & Drink

The V&A Café is one of the oldest museum restaurants in the world. There are also cafés and kiosks throughout the building.
